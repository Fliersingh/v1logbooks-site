Privacy Policy

V1 Logbooks (com.v1logbooks.app) · Last updated: 19 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data V1 Logbooks ("we", "us", "the App") collects, why we collect it, how it is used and protected, and the rights you have over it. V1 Logbooks is a personal flight logbook application built for pilots operating under the regulatory framework of the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA), India, and is used to record flight time, credentials, and — optionally — to prepare and submit entries to DGCA's official eGCA portal on your behalf, using your own DGCA login.

1. Data We Collect

Account & profile data

When you register, we collect:

  • Full name, date of birth, nationality
  • Email address and password (stored as a salted hash — we never store your plaintext password)
  • Your pilot type/role and, if applicable, your DGCA eGCA licence ID
  • Mobile phone number
  • Emergency contact name and phone number
  • Optionally, if you choose to add it in Settings: a profile photo
  • Your career stage and employment details you choose to record — for example your default operator/airline, and whether you are an instructor or captain — used to tailor currency and progress calculations to your situation
  • A timestamp and version marker recording when you accepted this Policy and our Terms, for our own compliance records

Flight logbook data

The core purpose of the App is to record your flights. This includes aircraft registration and type, departure/arrival aerodromes, dates and times, block/flight time, the role you flew in, and any purposes, exercises, or remarks you attach to a flight.

Credentials & currency data

Licence details, ratings, medical certificate data, and recurrent training records you choose to log, so the App can track their validity and expiry on your behalf.

Scanned logbook images

If you use the AI-assisted logbook scanner to import historical entries, photos of your logbook pages are processed to extract flight data (see Section 5 for how this is handled).

Device & local storage data

The App stores your data locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database (SQLCipher, with the encryption key held in your device's secure hardware-backed keystore where available). This local copy is synchronized with our servers so your logbook is available if you reinstall the App or use it on another device.

2. Data About Other People (Crew, Instructors, Examiners)

DGCA flight-time regulations require you to record who else was on board or supervising a flight — co-pilots, instructors, examiners, and the verifier who attests your entries. When you add these people to your logbook, we store their name, licence number where you provide it, and their eGCA ID where known. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to record another person's details — typically because you flew with them and DGCA record-keeping rules require it. We do not use this data for any purpose other than completing your logbook and, where you choose to submit a flight to eGCA, DGCA submission.

3. eGCA Pilot Directory Data

This section describes a feature currently under review. We are re-evaluating how much of this directory data needs to live on your device, and expect to narrow it to lookup-only in a future update. This policy will be updated when that change ships.

To make it faster to add crew you fly with to a flight entry, the App maintains a searchable directory of pilot names and eGCA IDs sourced from DGCA's own eGCA system. This directory may include names of individuals who are not registered users of V1 Logbooks. Only name, eGCA ID, and licence number (where available) are held — no contact details, flight history, or other personal data about non-users is collected or stored.

If you are a licensed pilot and would like your name removed from this directory, or have questions about this data, contact us using the details in Section 13.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and secure your account, and authenticate you (including one-time passcodes sent by email)
  • To store, sync, and back up your flight logbook across your devices
  • To calculate currency, recency, and regulatory totals (e.g. rolling 90-day/28-day/365-day limits) for your own reference
  • To prepare and, at your explicit request, submit flight entries to the DGCA eGCA portal using your own credentials
  • To send you account-related emails (verification codes, account notices)
  • To provide customer support

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

6. DGCA / eGCA Portal Integration

V1 Logbooks is an independent application and is not developed, endorsed, or operated by DGCA. When you choose to upload a flight to eGCA, the App opens DGCA's own official web portal inside the App and submits the form on your behalf using your DGCA login, exactly as if you had typed it into DGCA's website yourself. Your eGCA password is used only for that single login session — it is never saved to your device or sent to our servers, and is discarded from memory as soon as the session ends. Data you submit to DGCA through this feature becomes subject to DGCA's own data handling as the government record-holder.

7. Data Retention

  • Account & flight data: kept for as long as your account is active.
  • Scanned-image extraction results: not stored on our servers -- passed through to your device for review, then discarded server-side.
  • Backups: the App's backup/export feature generates a file that is saved to your own device, and a copy is also stored on our servers so it can be restored later. You can delete individual backup records at any time from the in-App backup history. Backup files are not yet encrypted at rest on our servers, so treat any copy you download or share like the rest of your flight record.
  • Account deletion: if you delete your account in Settings, we permanently and irreversibly delete your profile, flight logbook, credentials, crew records, and all associated data from our servers. This action cannot be undone. One narrow exception: we retain a minimal anti-abuse record — your DGCA eGCA ID and the date your free trial was used — with no flight or personal data attached, solely to prevent the same eGCA ID from being used to claim a new free trial after deleting and recreating an account.

8. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed (never stored in plaintext) using industry-standard, salted hashing.
  • Your local on-device data is encrypted at rest.
  • Data in transit between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access to your data on our servers is scoped so that only you (and, for eGCA submissions, DGCA) can access your own records.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your data.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (most fields are editable directly in the App)
  • Export your data via the in-App backup feature
  • Delete your account and associated data via the in-App account-deletion feature, or by contacting us (see the narrow anti-abuse exception in Section 7)
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer (Section 13) or the applicable data protection authority

10. Age Requirement

V1 Logbooks is intended for licensed and student pilots. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; if you believe a minor has registered an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (including Google, for AI-assisted logbook scanning, and our hosting/email providers) may process data outside India. Where this occurs, we rely on those providers' own contractual and technical safeguards for cross-border data transfer.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App or by email before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
